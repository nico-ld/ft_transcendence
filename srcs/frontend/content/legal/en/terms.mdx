## The service

{legalInfo.projectName} lets HR users (the app’s administrators) create, update and delete personal workspaces for employees. Each workspace is an isolated Linux environment with the tools for the employee’s job. Employees log in and use their workspace through a terminal in their web browser. Several users can use the service at the same time.

The service is free. It is a development and evaluation project: features may change without notice, and the service may be stopped, reset or deleted at any time, in particular at the end of the project.
